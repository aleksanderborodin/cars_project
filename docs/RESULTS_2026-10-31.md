## Score-only pilot: 20 reused development tracks per condition

This separate [declared pilot](OBJECTIVE_ALIGNMENT_2026-10-30.md) holds the model,
seven candidates, actor, horizon and control frequency fixed. Alpha scales only
internal nonprogress penalties; the official environment reward is unchanged.
Its direct controls differ from the 200-track means above because the panel is
smaller. Do not compare scores across these two panels as paired results.

| 0 | 863.55 | 885.70 | 899.09 | 881.19 |
| 1 | 880.05 | 860.90 | 865.47 | 821.67 |

Quarter penalties improve point return over original search by +13.39 on seed 0
and +4.57 on seed 1. Both descriptive paired 95% intervals cross zero:
[−1.07, 31.22] and [−21.45, 31.97]. Relative to direct execution, quarter penalties
give +35.54 [7.96, 69.53] and **−14.58 [−35.80, 5.28]**, respectively.
This is not an established repair. Removing penalties is worse than original
search for both actors; seed 1 loses 58.38 versus direct execution. Its mean
off-road sampled steps rise from 4.65 under original search to 62.75 without
penalties. Penalties therefore cannot simply be discarded on this evidence.

The declared screen requires point return above both direct and original search
for both actors. **Neither setting passes.** No wider validation is triggered.

## Integrity, costs and interpretation

The assistant verified every reported condition's metrics/config SHA256 against
its saved inputs: 30 main conditions and eight pilot summaries. Repository copies
of all six report/provenance files match the controllers' completed reports
byte-for-byte. The direct replay and full alpha1 parity gates passed; no negative
arm or failed actor was removed. Completed runs were not overwritten.

The main recovery ledger charges 5,216,072 native frames and 223,351,520 internal
physics ticks, including parity work and a conservative failed-attempt bound;
these are not exact costs for all historical reused panels. The pilot used
115,198 new native frames and 15,631,840 internal physics ticks, within its caps,
with zero new training. Operational recovery and overnight suspend remain in
their dated records. Both jobs finished and the temporary sleep guard released.
Raw timing with recorded software-check overlap does not establish clean latency.

The reports preserve exploratory/adaptive status: seed 0 and the pilot tracks
have been used repeatedly in development. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive,
not protection against adaptive selection or substitutes for independent actors.
No private ranking sidecar, held-out identity/result or final-test feedback was
used. Strong external driving baselines and controlled component studies remain
open; no perception, prediction or control subproblem is declared solved.

**Origin and next decision:** the user requested programmed simulation, separating
understanding from deciding, and actual-return improvement rather than proxy
success. The assistant designed the bounded penalty intervention and its screen;
simulation-based planning itself is established prior work. The measured result
rejects this intervention as a promoted repair. Next prepare competent independent
comparators and a separately declared diagnosis distinguishing scoring, model and
proposal limitations. Do not initiate a broader weight sweep or final test from
these results. Precise causal explanations remain uncertain.
